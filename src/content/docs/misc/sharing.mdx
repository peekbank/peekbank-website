Thank you so much for offering to share the data from your study with Peekbank!

## What data and materials should I share?

The primary data we are interested in is

<div class="ps-6 [&_ul]:list-none [&_ul]:ps-0 [&_ul_ul]:mt-1 [&_li]:ps-6 [&_li]:-indent-6">

- time course data
- with gazes to a target or distractor image
- in response to a target label
- recorded
  - at a regular sampling rate (e.g., AOI hits or coordinates captured via eye-tracker)
  - or on a frame-by-frame basis (e.g., looking side coded by a human).

</div>

We are also interested in information about the particular trial:

- what target and distractor objects/images are on the screen
- what target word and carrier phrase infants heard
- the timing of the target word onset
- the order of the trials

For participants, we are currently tracking some basic data:

- age
- sex
- native language

We'd especially welcome vocabulary information, e.g. MCDI or other language measures. We also welcome you to share the stimuli (audio, images, videos) used in the experiment.

Please also share the citation/reference you would like us to include for the dataset (e.g., doi or citation in APA format).

For a full overview of the data we are tracking, see our [codebook](../../peekbank/codebook/). To see examples of shared datasets, visit our <a href="https://stanford.redivis.com/datasets/frvk-5c922jwkx" target="_blank">Redivis file archive</a>.

## What format should the data be in?

Whatever format is easiest/most convenient for you to share! If multiple formats are possible, our preference is for "raw" data relative to pre-processed data. 

## What will happen with my data?

Once added to the Peekbank database, your data will be publicly available on Redivis and in the Peekbank public database. The data will be available both in your original format and in the standardized Peekbank data format. Users will be able to access your data via the [peekbankr](../../start/dataaccess/) API and the [interactive analyses](../../analyses/accuracy/) on this site.

## How do I get credit?

Our policy is that users must cite the original datasets when publishing or presenting work using the Peekbank database. Contributors to the database will also be referenced on the [How to Cite](../howtocite/) page of our website.

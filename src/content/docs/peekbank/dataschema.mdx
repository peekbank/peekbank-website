The Peekbank schema consists of a set of tables, each tracking different types of information about a given dataset. Each table can be accessed through functions in the <code>peekbankr</code> R package. Table columns are linked through connecting IDs (indicated by links in the schema figure below).

The schema changed with release 2025.1, so pick the one that matches the release you are working with.

<Tabs syncKey="schema">
<TabItem label="2025.1 onward">

- **datasets**: Information about the eyetracking dataset, including citation info
- **subjects**: Data on individual participants
- **administrations**: Properties of a specific session in which an individual subject participated
- **trial_types**: Properties of each trial, including the stimuli, target location, and carrier phrase.
- **stimuli**: Stimuli in the form of word-image pairs
- **trials**: One row per trial a subject completed, in order, with whether the original study excluded it
- **aoi_timepoints**: Participant looking to the areas of interest (target vs. distractor) sampled at regular time intervals
- **xy_timepoints**: Gaze coordinates of participants' looking to the screen sampled at regular time intervals (if available)
- **aoi_region_sets**: Positional information about areas of interest (if available)

Most tables also carry an `*_aux_data` column holding optional JSON, described in the [codebook](/peekbank/codebook/).

<TabItem label="2021.0 – 2022.1">

- **datasets**: Information about the eyetracking dataset, including citation info
- **subjects**: Data on individual participants
- **administrations**: Properties of a specific session in which an individual subject participated
- **trial_types**: Properties of each trial, including the stimuli, target location, and carrier phrase.
- **stimuli**: Stimuli in the form of word-image pairs
- **trials**: One row per trial type and position in a dataset, shared by every subject who saw that trial type at that position
- **aoi_timepoints**: Participant looking to the areas of interest (target vs. distractor) sampled at regular time intervals
- **xy_timepoints**: Gaze coordinates of participants' looking to the screen sampled at regular time intervals (if available)
- **aoi_region_sets**: Positional information about areas of interest (if available)

These releases have no `*_aux_data` columns, no trial exclusions and no `vanilla_trial` flag. Because a `trials` row means something different here, counts and joins on `trials` do not carry over to later releases unchanged.
